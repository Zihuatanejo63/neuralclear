NeuralClear

The clearing layer for agent-to-agent payments.

Reserve credits. Meter work. Settle net. Verify the receipt. A rail-agnostic evidence layer, not another payment rail.

Prototype. Internal escrow is a ledger reservation, not legal custody. Licensed partners must handle custody and compliance.

01 / CLEARING FLOW

From authorized work to auditable settlement.

Reserve

A mandate bounds spending; escrow reserves the agreed internal credits.

Meter

Each accepted entry links to its predecessor. Changes break the hash chain.

Settle net

Allocate metered charges and fees; return unused credits under the policy.

Sign & verify

Verify the receipt and metering chain without running a ledger.

Integrity is not truth: signatures and Merkle proofs authenticate records, not service quality. TEE/ZK execution verification is not implemented.

03 / COMPLEMENTARY LAYERS

Payment execution and clearing evidence are different jobs.

x402

HTTP-native payment requirements and execution around a request. A payment rail can settle the resulting obligation.

x402 documentation →

NeuralClear

Authorized budgets, verifiable metering and a net allocation with signed evidence. Complementary to payment rails, not a replacement.

Rail-agnostic is an architecture choice, not a list of completed integrations. Stripe has test evidence; production x402, bank and stablecoin adapters are not claimed here.
